01 / Origin

Started with one apple tree.

Happy Orchard began in 2018 on a 12-acre plot in the Western Ghats. A single apple tree, a homebrew kit, and an obsession with flavour — that's all it took to start a revolution in craft brewing.

🌱 12 Acres · Est. 2018
02 / Fruit

Over 40 varieties, zero shortcuts.

We grow Bramley apples, Morello cherries, Williams pears, and rare citrus hybrids. Every fruit is hand-picked at peak ripeness. No concentrates. No flavour syrups. Never.

🍎 40+ Fruit Varieties
03 / Process

Brewed slow. Fermented wild.

Each batch ferments for a minimum of 21 days in open-top copper vessels. We use wild ambient yeast captured from our own orchard. The result is something truly alive — complex, layered, alive.

⏱ 21-Day Fermentation
04 / Result

A beer that tastes like somewhere.

Every can of Happy Orchard carries the character of its land — the morning mist, the clay soil, the wildflowers. Terroir in a can. Crack one open and taste exactly where it came from.
